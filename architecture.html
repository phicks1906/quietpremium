The answer

Three views of the same spending.

One set of inputs. Three different questions. That separation is what keeps the theoretical ceiling from being mistaken for the decision you should actually make.

Current

What you capture now.

Your existing cards, spend routing, airline concentration, hotel behavior and travel choices — evaluated as they are today.

Maximum

The modeled ceiling.

The highest modeled value the same spending and travel behavior could produce when the structure is optimized for theoretical output.

Recommended

The Architecture to use.

The practical structure Quiet Premium would actually choose, balancing value, travel outcomes, status economics and simplicity.

Maximum is the ceiling. Recommended is the decision. Recommended can intentionally leave theoretical value on the table when a simpler structure or a better real-world travel experience makes that tradeoff worthwhile.
